How much US cloud can be seen in Swedish municipalities?

Email, accounts, websites and services at all 290 municipalities and 20 regions, measured from the outside with open signals. It shows configuration and requests, not how dependent the organisation is. The rules were written before the measurement, and every number can be checked.

    10receive email at Google (MX)
    284host their website with a Swedish or European provider

    What this does not show
    

      

    • Connection, not content. Email pointing to Microsoft in DNS says nothing about what information is stored there or whether it is confidential.
      • 

    • An account is not usage. A Microsoft tenant may exist only for Teams or a single service.
      • 

    • "No cloud signals" is not "no cloud". It only means the email setup does not reveal a provider from the outside.
      • 

    • One domain per organisation, one measurement. See the method (in Swedish).
      •

    Before consent: the website

      Security basics
      

      Basic protection in DNS and on the web. Reported only as totals, never per municipality, so the page does not become a list of targets.
      

      97have DMARC in monitoring mode (p=none) and do not ask receivers to stop spoofed email
      43 %reject email that spoofs the sender (DMARC p=reject)
      11 %have published MTA-STS for encrypted email to them
      83 %have a DNSSEC-signed domain
