The Swedish Tax Agency shows what the measurement cannot see

According to Swedish Radio's Ekot, the Swedish Tax Agency (Skatteverket) has adopted US cloud services on a large scale. Yet DNS shows email without cloud signals and the website makes no third-party requests. Microsoft's own email configuration service responds that the domain is registered in Exchange Online. 58 agency domains that do not appear as Microsoft in DNS are registered there. Absence of signals is not absence of cloud.
